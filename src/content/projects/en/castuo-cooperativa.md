## Audience

Two buyer personas, and every piece of the plan (ad, landing page, newsletter and post) targets one of them:

- **Álvaro**, a lover of authenticity who chooses exceptional-quality produce and connects with wine and the land.
- **Ana**, a lover of artisan, sustainable food who favours tradition and cooks consciously.

## SMART goals

Four goals, all over three months. They are the plan’s targets, not measured results.

| Goal | Target | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More followers and a presence on new networks | +20% (160 on Instagram and 606 on Facebook) | Social media content plan |
| Grow website sales | +20% conversions and sales | Google Ads (display & search) and Meta Ads |
| Build customer loyalty | 15% open rate and 8% click rate | A weekly newsletter per buyer persona |
| Launch a blog with good content | About 10 posts and 15% of website traffic | Keyword research and a calendar per audience |

## Context

An academic project for the Master’s in Digital Marketing and Web Design at CEI. The goal figures are the plan’s targets, not measured results.
