challenge: Castúo Cooperativa (Cooperativa del campo San Pedro) has an award-winning product and a story to tell, but little visibility outside its area. Its website, which is also its online shop, had incomplete content and SEO gaps, no blog and no newsletter, and in the competitor analysis it had the weakest figures for blog and social media, and the fewest backlinks and lowest Authority Score for its website.
solution: I analysed the company and three competing cooperatives (domain authority, backlinks, organic traffic, blog and social media), and built the SWOT, the CAME, the Business Model Canvas and two buyer personas. From there I set SMART goals and a plan for May, June and July with a website redesign and on-page and off-page SEO, Google Ads search and display ads, Meta ads, a new blog, a weekly newsletter and a social media calendar, with KPIs for each goal and a fixed budget.

A project for the Master’s in Digital Marketing and Web Design at CEI. These were the plan’s goals for a three-month period (they are goals, not measured results):

- Grow social media followers by at least 20% and start on new channels.
- Grow online shop sales by at least 20%.
- Build customer loyalty with a newsletter reaching a 15% open rate and an 8% click rate.
- Launch a blog that brings 15% of the website’s traffic.
