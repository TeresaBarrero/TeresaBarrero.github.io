## Who it's for

360º creative professionals, whether freelancers or small studios: branding, graphic and web design, development, social media, SEO and ads, copywriting, photo and video, and communication. nebúla brings together what is now spread across a CRM, a freelance tool, an agency tool and an invoicing tool.

## Built for non-technical people

- Plain language across the interface: “You're owed”, “You'll receive”, “They've paid me”, “Overdue”.
- “?” help tips with a one-line explanation for every technical term, such as VAT, income tax withholding or corrective invoice.
- Simple mode by default, with advanced options behind “More options”.
- Everything linked: contact, company, opportunity, project, tasks, hours, deliverables, invoices and contract.

## From brief to payment

- Client portal: from a single link, the client signs the contract, approves deliverables in review rounds and pays online.
- Connected to Google Calendar: meetings show up on the home screen and in the calendar.
- Installable web app on phone and computer (PWA), with offline access.

## Design and development

I designed a custom visual system, pastel, clean and organised, with light and dark mode. I started from a working prototype and a full specification of screens, data and rules, and led the development in phases with Next.js, TypeScript and Supabase, with data hosted in the EU.
