## Website

A static landing page in the brand’s charcoal and turquoise, with activities, prices, timetable and news. The design starts on mobile (390 px) and everything leads to WhatsApp, the only contact channel.

- Custom form with a database and a private panel for the owner.
- Granular cookie banner and GA4 in Consent Mode v2, with three conversion events.
- No external libraries.

## Social media

Instagram and Facebook community management for the launch: building a local community before opening, filling the open days and explaining timetables, prices and the booking app.

- Instagram brand foundations: tone, pillars and visual style.
- Pre-opening calendar.
- Facebook plan.
- Pieces in the same visual line as the website: equipment photos, bold type and turquoise as the accent.

## Local SEO & GEO

So that anyone looking for a gym in Guareña finds Panthera, on Google, on Maps and in AI assistants.

- SEO audit of the website.
- Google Business Profile listing.
- ExerciseGym structured data (address, opening hours, prices and social profiles).
- Sitemap and llms.txt file so AI search engines understand the business.
